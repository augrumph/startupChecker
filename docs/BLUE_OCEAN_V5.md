# Blue Ocean module — Thesis Engine V5

This layer is mandatory for every researched thesis but **cannot rescue weak core economics or utility**.

## Research questions

1. What is the real competitive arena?
2. Which factors does the industry compete on today?
3. Who are the three tiers of noncustomers?
4. Where are the buyer-utility blocks?
5. ERRC: what should be eliminated, reduced, raised and created?
6. Does the proposal simultaneously improve buyer utility and cost structure?
7. Is there evidence of latent demand, or is this merely an empty ocean?

## Deterministic signals

- `value_curve_departure` — 20%
- `noncustomer_unlock` — 20%
- `utility_leap` — 20%
- `cost_curve_break` — 15%
- `new_demand_creation` — 15%
- `latent_demand_evidence` — 10%

## Classification

- `BLUE_VALIDATED` — strong score + high evidence + value innovation gates
- `BLUE_HYPOTHESIS` — promising value innovation, still needs evidence
- `PURPLE_OCEAN` — meaningful differentiated wedge inside an existing market
- `RED_OCEAN` — mostly competing on established factors
- `EMPTY_OCEAN_RISK` — weak demand evidence despite apparent white space

## Guardrail

No-competition is not a positive signal by itself.

The Blue Ocean score is informational and does not change the Rust decision gates for payer, ability to pay, value intensity or value-price surplus.
